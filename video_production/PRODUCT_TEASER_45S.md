## 🎬 Teaser Production Specifications
- **Target Runtime:** 45 seconds (00:00 - 00:45)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (1920×1080) / Vertical 9:16 adaptable
- **Pacing:** Dynamic, high-energy SaaS launch pacing with calm resolution
- **Music:** Light, rhythmic ambient electronic beat building to an uplifting chord resolution
- **Voiceover Artist:** Confident, warm, articulate English narration

## ⏱️ Beat-by-Beat Teaser Storyboard

### Beat 1: The Fragmentation Hook (00:00 – 00:08)
- **Visual:** Fast, cinematic montage of chaotic preparation: paper scraps with scribbled questions, a ringing phone calendar alert, a prescription bottle with tiny text, and a sticky note lost in a bag.
- **On-Screen Text:** "Important appointments. Scattered preparation."
- **Voiceover:** *"How many times have you walked out of a doctor's appointment wishing you’d remembered to ask that one crucial question?"*
- **SFX:** Soft paper rustle, subtle calendar alert ping.

### Beat 2: The Solution Reveal (00:08 – 00:16)
- **Visual:** Smooth cinematic transition. The scattered notes pull inward with a subtle magnetic snap into a clean, floating dashboard with calm sage-green styling. The Appointment Prep logo reveals with an elegant shimmer.
- **On-Screen Text:** "Meet Appointment Prep."
- **Voiceover:** *"Meet Appointment Prep. One private workspace to organize your medical visits with clarity and confidence."*
- **SFX:** Clean digital whoosh, uplifting synth intro.

### Beat 3: The 3-Step Power Workflow (00:16 – 00:32)
- **Visual 1 (00:16 - 00:21):** Screen moves into the **Question Organiser**. Alex Morgan types: *"What points should I discuss?"* with prioritized drag handles and asked/unasked status toggles.
  - *Text:* "1. Prioritize Questions"
- **Visual 2 (00:21 - 00:26):** Screen transitions into **Medication Tracker**. Fictional entry *"Example Medication A — 10mg once daily"* appears cleanly preserved verbatim.
  - *Text:* "2. Log Medications Verbatim"
- **Visual 3 (00:26 - 00:32):** User clicks the green **"Preparation Checklist"** button. An animated document unfolds into the 4-section structured layout.
  - *Text:* "3. One-Click Clinical Checklist"
- **Voiceover:** *"Organize the questions you want answered. Log your self-reported medications. And with one click, generate a concise, printable clinical checklist ready for the exam room."*
- **SFX:** Subtle UI pop, card slide, satisfying print chime.

### Beat 4: Physical Outcome & Privacy (00:32 – 00:38)
- **Visual:** The web interface smoothly transforms into a clean physical printed page sitting on a wooden desk next to a pen. Browser print preview shows single-page perfection.
- **On-Screen Text:** "Zero clinical diagnosis. 100% patient empowerment."
- **Voiceover:** *"No automated clinical diagnoses. No altered prescriptions. Just your thoughts, your medications, and your questions—ready when it matters most."*
- **SFX:** Subtle paper drop, soft camera focal shift.

### Beat 5: Outro & Call to Action (00:38 – 00:45)
- **Visual:** Elegant sage-green background with the official brand badge and live link: `https://backendcia2.vercel.app`.
- **On-Screen Text:**
  - **Appointment Prep**
  - *"Prepare better. Remember what matters. Stay organised."*
  - **Try Live: backendcia2.vercel.app**
- **Voiceover:** *"Appointment Prep. Prepare better. Remember what matters. Stay organised."*
- **SFX:** Gentle musical fade-out with warm acoustic bass chord.
